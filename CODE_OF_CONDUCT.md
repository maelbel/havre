# Code of Conduct

Havre follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
the code of conduct used by most open-source projects. In short:

## Our standards

Everyone taking part — in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — is expected to:

- be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's experience, background or identity;
- give and accept constructive feedback about the work, never about the person;
- assume good faith, and take responsibility for mistakes;
- respect people's privacy: never share someone's personal information (an email address, a screenshot of a
  mailbox) without their permission.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualised language or imagery, trolling, discriminatory
jokes, and publishing others' private information.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions) and when someone
represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer (the contact on their GitHub profile). Reports are
reviewed promptly and confidentially. Depending on what happened, the maintainer may give a warning, hide or remove
content, or ban someone temporarily or permanently, following the
[Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 is the reference: in case of doubt, it applies.
